      How it works

      1. Read your taste

      Each favourite is resolved to a Qloo entity, then we pull the tags they share — that's your fingerprint. "Dark humour, slow burn, moral grey areas" says more than "drama".

      2. Cross the language line

      The agent asks Qloo's Insights API for films and series released in the target country, and artists, books and podcasts carrying the right language tags — all ranked by affinity to your favourites.

      3. Check & retry

      Empty result? Wrong language crept in? The agent loosens popularity, swaps country formats, or turns a hard filter into a soft signal, and tells you what it did.

      4. Make it a habit

      Results become a four-week ladder matched to your level: music first for beginners, books and native podcasts for advanced learners, and a real place to go order food.
